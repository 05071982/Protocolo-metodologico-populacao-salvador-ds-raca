## Métodos demográficos e estatísticos

1. Siegel JS, Swanson DA, editors. The methods and materials of demography. 2nd ed. San Diego: Elsevier Academic Press; 2004.
2. Smith SK, Tayman J, Swanson DA. A practitioner's guide to state and local population projections. Dordrecht: Springer; 2013.
3. Swanson DA, Tayman J. Subnational population estimates. Dordrecht: Springer; 2012.
4. Shryock HS, Siegel JS. The methods and materials of demography. Washington (DC): U.S. Bureau of the Census; 1973.
5. Hamilton CH, Perry J. A short method for projecting population by age from one decennial census to another. Soc Forces. 1962;41(2):163-70.
6. Deming WE, Stephan FF. On a least squares adjustment of a sampled frequency table when the expected marginal totals are known. Ann Math Stat. 1940;11(4):427-44.
7. Bishop YMM, Fienberg SE, Holland PW. Discrete multivariate analysis: theory and practice. Cambridge (MA): MIT Press; 1975.
8. Rao JNK, Molina I. Small area estimation. 2nd ed. Hoboken (NJ): Wiley; 2015.
9. Burden RL, Faires JD. Análise numérica. Tradução da 8ª ed. norte-americana. São Paulo: Cengage Learning; 2008.
10. Runge C. Über empirische Funktionen und die Interpolation zwischen äquidistanten Ordinaten. Z Math Phys. 1901;46:224-43.
11. Balinski ML, Young HP. Fair representation: meeting the ideal of one man, one vote. New Haven: Yale University Press; 1982.

*A interpolação osculatória de Sprague para desagregação de faixas quinquenais está descrita em Siegel & Swanson (2004), ref. 1, e Shryock & Siegel (1973), ref. 4.*
